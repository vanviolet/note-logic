### Architecture Decisions
- Active track's notes = the "single source of truth" for the grid/sidebar (no dual state)
- Multi-track AlphaTex only generated when >1 track (performance: avoids multi-track overhead for most users)
- Track volumes applied via alphaTab API's `changeTrackVolume` per-track method
- `useDeferredValue` applied to the full tracks array for alphaTex computation to keep grid responsive
