### Comprehensive Materi Page (~1300 lines)
Created `materi.tsx` with 11 sections covering ALL music notation topics:
1. **Paranada (Staff)** — 5 lines, 4 spaces, ledger lines, grand staff
2. **Kunci (Clef)** — Treble, Bass, Alto, Tenor with descriptions
3. **Posisi Not** — Line/space positions for treble & bass clef + mnemonics
4. **Accidental** — Sharp, Flat, Natural, Double Sharp/Flat + enharmonic equivalents
5. **Durasi Not** — Lazy-loaded DurationGuide component (VexFlow)
6. **Time Signature** — 4/4, 3/4, 2/4, 6/8, cut time with beat visualizations
7. **Key Signature** — Sharp/flat key tables + FCGDAEB/BEADGCF mnemonics
8. **Circle of Fifths** — Full SVG diagram showing 12 major + 12 minor keys
9. **Dinamika** — pp through ff + crescendo/decrescendo
10. **Artikulasi** — 9 types: staccato, legato, accent, tenuto, fermata, tie, marcato, slur, trill
11. **Tanda Tempo** — 10 Italian terms with BPM + tempo change terms

Features: Sticky TOC sidebar with IntersectionObserver-based scroll tracking, responsive layout.
